ShotSyncCam Support

ShotSyncCam is a companion app for Wi-Fi body cameras. Currently supported hardware: VIEVU LE4.

FAQ

Can't connect? Make sure the camera's Wi-Fi is on (network name begins with LE4-), and allow Location and Local Network access in Settings › ShotSyncCam.

Where are my clips? In the app's Library, and in the Files app under On My iPhone › ShotSyncCam.

Camera time wrong? Open Status and tap Sync Clock.

Will other cameras work? Not yet; more camera profiles may be added.

For help, email us and include your iPhone model and iOS version.
