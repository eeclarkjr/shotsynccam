ShotSyncCam Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026.

ShotSyncCam does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Permissions

  • Location: used only to read the current Wi-Fi network name so the app can tell whether you are connected to your camera. Your location is never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Local Network: used only to communicate with your camera over its Wi-Fi.

Your files

Photos and clips you pull from your camera are stored only on your device. They leave it only if you choose to share them.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
