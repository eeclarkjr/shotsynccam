Minyl Support

Minyl catalogs your vinyl record collection: scan a barcode, read a label, or search MusicBrainz, then keep your pressings, conditions, shelves, and photos in one place.

FAQ

A record has no barcode? In Add Record, choose Text and photograph the cover or center label. Minyl reads the text on your iPhone and searches MusicBrainz, or you can enter it by hand.

MusicBrainz doesn't have my pressing? Use Manual entry. You can add the tracklist yourself.

Photos come out sideways or with the floor in them? Minyl crops to the sleeve's edges and turns text upright automatically. Drag the corners or use the rotate buttons to adjust. Auto-rotate can be turned off in the More tab.

Syncing between devices? Sign in to iCloud on each device and keep iCloud on for Minyl in Settings › your name › iCloud.

Apple Music? It's optional. Subscribers can play records and save party playlists; everyone else can open albums in Apple Music.

Export? Open the More tab and tap Prepare CSV export.

For help, email us and include your device model and iOS version.
