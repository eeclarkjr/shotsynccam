Minyl Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026.

Minyl does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking. The developer cannot see your collection.

Your collection

Your records, notes, and photos are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, they sync through your own private iCloud database, which only you can access.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only to scan barcodes and to photograph your own sleeves, labels, and back covers. Photos are processed on your device, including text recognition and cropping.
  • Apple Music (optional): used to match your records, play them, and save party playlists. Minyl works without Apple Music.
  • Photos (optional): used only when you choose a photo or save one.

Online lookups

When you search or scan a barcode, Minyl sends the search terms or barcode to MusicBrainz to find release details. No personal information or identifiers are sent. Apple Music requests are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Links to YouTube or Spotify open those services only when you tap them.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
